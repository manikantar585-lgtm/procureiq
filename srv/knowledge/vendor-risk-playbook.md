# Vendor fraud & risk playbook (Internal Audit)

## Red flags
1. Bank account changed on the invoice or by email request.
2. Brand-new vendor sending high-value invoices quickly after onboarding.
3. Invoices clustered just below an approval limit, or several invoices in a short period that together exceed it (split invoicing).
4. Invoice number already used, or the same amount billed twice.
5. Billing for quantities that were never received (check GR in SAP).
6. Prices drifting above the contracted PO price.
7. GSTIN on invoice not belonging to the vendor (possible fake invoice for ITC fraud).
8. Round-figure amounts and invoices dated on Sundays or public holidays.

## Response
- Critical: keep the invoice blocked, inform Internal Audit within 24 hours, freeze vendor payments if a bank change is involved.
- High: buyer and AP Lead review within 2 working days; request credit note or corrected invoice.
- Medium: approver reviews the specific signal and documents the reason when approving.
